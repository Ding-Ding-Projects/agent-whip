Site settings

These preferences apply to this documentation site only. They're stored in your browser's local storage — nothing here is sent anywhere.

    Appearance

    Theme
    Light, dark, or match your system.
    Density
    Compact to comfortable spacing across tabs and lists.

    Accent colour

    An infinite colour picker: drag the field and hue slider, or type exact values. This recolours the site's primary accent (buttons, selected tab, the colour swatch below) in both light and dark.

    Notifications

    Test a notification
    Non-blocking toast, bottom-right, with a reviewable history.

    Tab strip

    Dock the navigation strip to any edge — the controls are at the top of the strip itself. Your choice persists across visits.

    What isn't available here

    This is a static, self-contained site with no backend. Account sign-in, synced settings across devices, and server-rendered search are not offered — everything above runs entirely in your browser and resets if you clear site data. Language modes and the TOTP authenticator described in the shared agent instructions apply to the agent-whip application itself, not to this static documentation site, and are not duplicated here.
